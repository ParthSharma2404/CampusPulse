How CampusPulse Works

No paper complaint registers or waiting at facility offices. Everything is managed digitally in 3 rapid steps.

01

1. Spot & Snap

Snap a photo of the defect right from your mobile camera. Pick your classroom, laboratory, or hostel room from intuitive pickers, or specify a custom spot.

● Instant Camera Capture
02

2. Hotspot Clustering

If multiple students report an issue in the same room (e.g. 5 reports for AC water leaking in LH-3), CampusPulse clusters them into one ticket and auto-escalates priority to URGENT.

● Smart Deduplication
03

3. Real-Time Push Alerts

Once the facility team completes the fix, they submit resolution notes and broadcast. Every student who reported that defect receives an instant live chime and screen toast.

● Real-Time SSE Broadcast
